This .uf2 file is not a picture

.uf2 files are firmware for a Raspberry Pi Pico. Plugged into the Switch, the Pico acts as a controller and draws the design in the game by pressing buttons.

There is no image inside to import. Look for the PNG or JPEG of the design, or load the .uf2 onto a Pico as its author intended.
